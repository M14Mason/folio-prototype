Is my work used to train AI?

No. Folio has a strict zero-data-retention policy, and your artwork is never used to train third-party AI models.

What happens when I add new work?

Drop a file into a subfolder and it appears in that tab. Starter publishes when you press update, up to twice a month. Automate checks your folder and updates the site on its own twice a week.

What does the $20 cover?

Registering your own domain and setting it up, paid once. You can also stay on a free folio.site address.

Can I sell prints?

Yes, if you opt in. You set the price; printing, framing and shipping are handled through print on demand.
